# Independent audit of the single-packet Q-curve argument

Write \(a=w_n,\ b=8u_n^2v_n^2\), so that \(w_{2n}=a^4-2b^2\), with \(a\) odd, \(b\) even and \(\gcd(a,b)=1\). An assumption \(w_{2n}=z^q\) can be restricted to primes \(q\ge151\) by the separate periodic certificates. The checks below support exclusion also in this remaining range for every dyadic \(n\ge8\). The initial \(w_8=2273\) is prime. [Small-exponent certificate](abc_dyadic_cm_power_characters.md) · [Local and full Hecke-space calculation](abc_dyadic_single_power_audit.py)

* Put \(K=\mathbb Q(s),s^2=2\), \(g=a^2+bs\), and \(C:y^2=x^3+2asx^2+gx\). The quotient by \((0,0)\) has coefficients \(-4as,4\bar g\). Dividing its coordinates by \(s^2,s^3\) yields \(\mu:C\to{}^\sigma C\). The normalized quotient has multiplier 1 on differentials and the scaling has multiplier \(s\). Therefore \({}^\sigma\mu\circ\mu=[-2]\), by its differential in characteristic zero.
* Over \(L=\mathbb Q(\kappa)\), \(\kappa^2=-2\), the two constituents of the induced Galois representation differ by \(\chi_8\), the character of \(K/\mathbb Q\). This follows by extending \(V_q(C)\) from \(G_K\) with the conjugate isogeny divided by \(\kappa\) off \(G_K\): the relation just computed gives the cocycle identity, and changing the sign of \(\kappa\) changes just that coset. On the exterior square the multiplier of the isogeny is 2 and that of the scalar is \(\kappa^{-2}\). Thus \(\det\rho=\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}\chi_8\). The character \(\chi_8\) is even, so the representation is odd. This proves the determinant directly without an assumption on the Hecke field.
* At every odd place the ideals \((g),(\bar g)\) are coprime, and their product is \((z^q)\). The model has \(\Delta=64g^2\bar g,\ c_4=16(5a^2-3bs)\). At each odd prime of bad reduction the latter is a unit, giving minimal multiplicative reduction with \(q\mid v(\Delta_{\min})\). Away from \(q\) this kills residual inertia by the Tate-curve description. At \(q\), good reduction or multiplicative reduction with \(q\mid v(\Delta_{\min})\) makes \(C[q]\) finite flat: in the latter case the Tate parameter is represented by a unit modulo \(q\)-th powers, after an unramified extension in the nonsplit case. At odd primes \(K/\mathbb Q\) is unramified. Weil restriction of a finite flat model along an unramified local extension is finite flat, and a Galois subquotient of its generic fiber is finite flat by schematic closure and quotient. This also applies after extending the residual coefficient field, inside a finite direct sum of copies. Thus the residual constituents are finite flat at \(q\).
* At the place above 2, \(v_s(\Delta)=12\). Ogg's formula bounds the local conductor of \(C\) by \(v_s(\Delta_{\min})\le12\). The conductor induction formula bounds the exponent for the full Weil restriction at 2 by \(2\cdot3+12=18\). The two constituent conductors agree even when \(q\) splits in \(L\). Indeed \(\rho\otimes\chi_8=\rho^\vee\otimes\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}\), and the cyclotomic character is unramified at 2. Duality preserves the Artin conductor. Hence the conductor exponent of each constituent is at most 9, also after mod-\(q\) reduction and semisimplification.
* At 47 the halves of the indexing process use the orbit starting \(4R=(32,11)\), then \((32,36)\), then \((32,11)\). Neither ordinate vanishes. At both states \(b/a^2=8(x/y)^2=4\bmod47\); \(a\) is a unit. Twisting \(C\) by \(1/a\) gives \(y^2=x^3+2sx^2+(1+4s)x\) on reduction, of trace \(-2\) for **both** \(s=7,40\bmod47\). All curves have good reduction there. The trace of the actual constituent is therefore \(2\) or \(-2\).
* If the residual constituent is absolutely irreducible, the weight and conductor conclusions of Serre modularity give weight two and level \(N\mid512\), by the finite-flat case just checked. The character is exactly \(\chi_8\): its reduction is fixed by the determinant and the group of characters at level 512 has order a power of two. All smaller levels occur as oldforms at level 512. PARI on the **entire** 48-dimensional cusp space \(S_2(512,\chi_8)\) gives
\[
  \det(X-T_{47})=X^{36}(X-8)^4(X+8)^4(X^2-128)^2.
\]
Thus the absolute determinants of \(T_{47}\pm2\) are both \(2^{48}3^45^4 31^2\), excluding every \(q\ge151\).
* If it is reducible over \(\overline{\mathbb F}_q\), the two characters have \(q\)-inertial restrictions 1 and \(\bar\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}\) by finite flatness over \(\mathbb Q_q\) and the determinant. After removing the requisite cyclotomic powers they are two Dirichlet characters \(\varepsilon_i\) unramified away from 2, with product \(\chi_8\) and sum of 2-conductor exponents at most 9. If one exponent exceeds 3, both are equal, because cancellation to conductor 8 is required. Hence both conductors divide 16. Since \(47\equiv-1\bmod16\) and \(\chi_8(47)=1\), their values at 47 are equal signs. The trace is \(\pm48\bmod q\); comparison with \(\pm2\) excludes every \(q\ge151\).

Excluding all prime exponents proves that the gcd of the complete set of prime valuations of \(w_n\) is 1. This alone leaves open a set of multiplicities all exceeding one.
