In every row the verifier proves primality by trial division, checks [r]G=O, checks all the lower multiples displayed are affine, and verifies the orientation by addition over the prime field. This proves exact rational order r. As α has squarefree norm r, the ℤ[i]-annihilator is exactly (α) at the chosen embedding: its quotient of ℤ[i]/(α), which already has size r, contains the cyclic subgroup generated by G of order r. ## Whole denominator factors certified without complete factorization

For α=a+bi, write [a]G=(x,y) and [b]G=(u,v) over the rationals. Addition to (−u,iv) gives the real and imaginary parts of the resulting abscissa as
\[
 \frac{y^2-v^2}{(x+u)^2}-x+u,\qquad -\frac{2yv}{(x+u)^2}.
\]
Take the lcm of the two reduced rational denominators, remove all powers of 2 and 3, and take its exact integer square root B. The code computes B also for every immediate Gaussian divisor α/λ. Remove from B the entire support of 6r and of all those lower denominators by repeated gcd. Call the remaining integer N.

At r=65 the two N values factor completely as
\[
 277\cdot23423797\cdot189458413,\qquad
 229\cdot6661\cdot313933\cdot21235213.
\]
At r=1105 the four N values have 296, 297, 297 and 297 decimal digits in table order. The verifier proves directly that they are pairwise coprime. For each N it additionally verifies ψᵣ(G)≡0 modulo N and gcd(N,ψ_{r/ℓ}(G))=1 for each prime ℓ dividing r. Since every p dividing N is a good prime for E₆ and 2y(G)=18 is a unit, all those p have exact order r, including the unfactored parts. Every listed prime has exponent one in its N.
