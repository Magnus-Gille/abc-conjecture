Date: 2026-07-26

I checked the primary PDF of Martin--Miao, arXiv:1409.2974, rather than
the search extraction.  Section 2.4, p. 6, lines 357--360 of the PDF text
**does list our exact identity**

\[
  (b-a)^2+4ab=c^2.
\]

It is one item in their list of polynomial transfers.  They later iterate a
different transfer, not this one.  So the precise priority position is:

- exact transfer identity: published prior art (Martin--Miao);
- iteration of this exact transfer to create infinitely many hits: explicitly
  stated in the 2020 MathOverflow answer
  <https://mathoverflow.net/questions/356295/how-balanced-can-abc-triples-be>;
- the general-seed prime-support formula, exact telescoped radical identity,
  effective Baker bound, and aggregate quality equivalence: no match found in
  our bounded searches.

The exact triple also occurs much earlier in Oesterlé, *Nouvelles approches du
“théorème” de Fermat*, Séminaire Bourbaki 694, Astérisque 161--162 (1988),
165--186, p. 170 (used in the abc/Szpiro argument):
<https://www.numdam.org/item/SB_1987-1988__30__165_0/>.
